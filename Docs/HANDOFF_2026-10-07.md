## Verified progress

- Card authoring/presentation: `CardData`, modular embedded effect slots,
  `CardView`, visual library, monster and spell/trap prefabs, sample cards.
- Pure core: `CardDefinition`, per-copy `CardInstance`/`instanceId`,
  `GameConfig`, seeded shuffle/starting hands, two players, deck/hand/zones,
  Recovery → Standby → Main → Battle → End, Energy, summon/tribute/position,
  combat, Graveyard, damage, and winner detection.
- Actions go through `GameSession`/`GameRules`; valid actions are logged and
  emit `GameEvent` values. `EnterEndPhase` goes directly from Main or Battle
  to End. Step/phase skips are assigned to exact match turns and repeated
  skips for the same turn do not become future skipped turns.
- Normal Spell path: Hand → empty Spell/Trap Zone face-up → pay Energy and
  resolve Draw Cards → Graveyard face-down; smoke-tested by the student.
- Reusable Skip Next Opponent Draw resolution: computes the affected player's
  next unresolved Draw Step, whether activated on the user's or opponent's
  turn. On 7 October the student reported all lines passing in `Run Skip
  Opponent Draw Smoke Test`, including actually skipped draws and a later
  normal draw. This is **not** a playable Trap/response action yet.
- On 7 October the student also reported passes for `Run Direct End Phase
  Smoke Test`, `Run Recovery Skip Smoke Test`, and `Run Effect Pipeline Smoke
  Test`. Earlier Main, Battle, initialization, and Normal Spell smoke tests
  were reported passing in the conversation.
- The portrait board layout, HUD, Deck/Graveyard/Field/Hand zones, and seeded
  state preview exist. The preview uses sample repeated cards, not finished
  decks. The phase-control code submits phase actions and auto-resolves empty
  Recovery/Standby, but the student has **not yet reported a Play-mode click
  test of that new phase-button wiring**. Card-selection actions are not bound.
