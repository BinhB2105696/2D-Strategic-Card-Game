# Card artwork progress

Direction: primarily mature dramatic 2D fantasy card illustration matching the approved Fire Dragon artwork: sharp anatomy/contours, detailed shading, strong elemental lighting. Earlier cartoon references are secondary; landscape 4:3, no embedded card text/frame.
Female humanoid Spellcasters and male humanoid Warriors are general initial directions; Sky Sage is explicitly an ELDERLY MALE sage per user correction. Individual names/concepts can override the initial gender direction.
Workflow: preview first; copy artwork into the project only after explicit user approval. Do not save prompt files. Vary poses and framing more boldly: subject must dominate >50% of the frame; moderately complex supporting backgrounds are allowed. Sky Sage is an elderly male Spellcaster. Expressions: natural, relaxed and appropriate; no forced smiles.
